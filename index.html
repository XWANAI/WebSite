About Us

Making intelligent robotics accessible to everyone.

Agent and Robot Company was founded with a simple belief: robots should enrich people's lives, not complicate them. We build autonomous systems that bridge the gap between digital intelligence and physical presence.

Our platforms power robots in over 70 countries, serving enterprises, educational institutions, and developers who are shaping the future of human-robot interaction.

  • Global presence with partners in 70+ countries
  • Open platform — developer-friendly SDKs and APIs
  • Privacy-first architecture with enterprise-grade security
  • Accessible in China and worldwide with full localization
  • Sustainable, long-life hardware designed for continuous operation
